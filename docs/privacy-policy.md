# Catalyst — Privacy Policy

*Draft for review. Last updated: 3 September 2026.*

> **Note before publishing:** this draft describes the app as it is built today. Have it reviewed before it goes live, and update it before any change that sends data off the device (accounts, syncing, or the consented research export).

## The short version

Catalyst runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. Your progress, your answers, and your name (if you gave one) stay on your phone and are deleted when you delete the app or reset it from the Account tab.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- **A first name**, if you choose to enter one, used only for greetings.
- **Your learning progress**: which units and lessons you have completed, checkpoint results, and streaks.
- **Your answers**: which questions you attempted, whether they were right, and what kind of mistake was made when they were wrong. This is used to build the app's recommendations and progress screens.
- **Settings**: narration, celebrations, reminder preferences, and an optional exam date you can set yourself.
- **Saved molecules** you build in the sandbox.

None of this leaves the device. There is no account, so there is nothing to sign in to and nothing stored about you elsewhere.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect analytics or usage statistics.
- It does not use advertising or any third-party tracking.
- It does not access your contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone.
- It does not require an internet connection to work.

## Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder in Account, the app schedules a notification on your device at the time you choose. This is done locally; no notification service outside your phone is involved. You can switch it off at any time, and it is off by default.

## Narration

The narrator uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

## Printing worksheets

If you print a practice set, a PDF is created on your device and passed to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from there is under your control.

## Children

Catalyst is designed for senior secondary students, some of whom are under 18. Because the app collects nothing off the device, there is no personal information about any user held by the developer. If you are a parent or guardian with questions, contact [support email].

## Research use

Catalyst is intended to support an educational research study. **No data is currently collected for research.** If a study proceeds, participation will require explicit, informed consent (and parental consent for participants under 18), will be covered by ethics approval, and will involve a separate, clearly described export step that the participant initiates. This policy will be updated before any such feature is enabled.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app, or use **Account → Reset all data**, which erases everything the app has stored on the device and returns it to first-time setup.

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
